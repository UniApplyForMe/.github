# UniApplyForMe NPC

**Every learner deserves a fair shot at higher education.**

UniApplyForMe is a South African non-profit (NPC · 2021/649773/08) that guides Grade 12 learners through university applications, NSFAS funding, bursary matching, and career decisions — from one place, at no cost to the learner.

---

## Our mission

South Africa's higher-education application process is fragmented, confusing, and often inaccessible to learners who need the most help. We build technology and provide hands-on support to change that — simplifying every step from choosing a degree to securing funding, so that a learner's postcode never determines their potential.

---

## What we do

**University application management** · We help learners identify the right institutions and programmes, prepare their applications, and track every submission through to an offer.

**NSFAS guidance** · Navigating NSFAS can be daunting. Our tools and advisors walk learners through eligibility checks, document uploads, and appeal processes.

**Bursary matching** · We surface relevant private bursaries and corporate funding opportunities that learners would otherwise miss, and help them apply.

**Career guidance** · Subject-choice decisions in Grade 10–12 shape futures. Our career-guidance resources help learners connect what they love to where they can go.

## Get involved

We are a small team with an ambitious goal, and we welcome people who want to make a real difference in South African education.

- **Developers** — whether you work in React, Next.js, backend APIs, or DevOps, there is meaningful work here. Open an issue or reach out to discuss where you can plug in.
- **Volunteers & advisors** — education counsellors, NSFAS-fluent individuals, and career coaches can contribute directly to learner outcomes without writing a single line of code.
- **Partners & sponsors** — universities, corporates with bursary programmes, and EdTech organisations: let's talk about how we can work together to reach more learners.

Browse our public repositories, open an issue, or contact us through [apply.org.za](https://apply.org.za).
