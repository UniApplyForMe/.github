# UniApplyForMe

**Think beyond the application!**

UniApplyForMe is a South African non-profit that helps Grade 12 learners navigate university applications, NSFAS funding, bursary matching, and career decisions, at no cost to the learner.

## Our mission

South Africa's higher-education application process is fragmented and often out of reach for the learners who need the most help. We build technology and provide hands-on support to change that, so that a learner's postcode never determines their potential.

## What we do

**University application management** We help learners identify the right institutions and programmes, prepare their applications, and track every submission through to an offer.

**NSFAS guidance** Our tools and advisors walk learners through eligibility checks, document uploads, and appeal processes.

**Bursary matching** We surface relevant private bursaries and corporate funding opportunities that learners would otherwise miss, and help them apply.

**Career guidance** Subject choices in Grade 10 to 12 shape futures. Our resources help learners connect what they love to where they can go.

## Get involved

**Developers** We offer a public API at [console.labs.org.za](https://console.labs.org.za) for anyone building tools that serve South African learners. For anything else, reach us at [info@apply.org.za](mailto:info@apply.org.za).

**Volunteers and advisors** Education counsellors, NSFAS-fluent individuals, and career coaches can contribute directly to learner outcomes without writing a single line of code. Get in touch at [info@apply.org.za](mailto:info@apply.org.za).

**Partners and sponsors** Universities, corporates with bursary programmes, and EdTech organisations are welcome to get in touch at [info@apply.org.za](mailto:info@apply.org.za).
